## Notes
- **Template families.** Nine files share one shape: `helpers/ordered-collection-boundary.ts` (2,674 lines) drives a binary search for the exact signed-Cardano cardinality boundary, then `exerciseMidgardOrderedCollectionBoundary` folds the Midgard field and `helpers/retained-da-boundary.ts` (574 lines) round-trips it through both DA classifications. The audit of every one of those files depends on those two helpers being correct; several decisive checks live inside them as `throw`s rather than in the test body (see retained-da-boundary.test.ts's row). Neither helper has a test of its own in this chunk.
- **Two publication channels, only one of them machine-checked.** `helpers/aiken-vector-channel.ts` writes a vector file only when `MIDGARD_WRITE_AIKEN_VECTOR=<dir>` is set; `scripts/generate-ordered-collection-boundary-aiken-goldens.mjs` sets it and CI runs that generator's `--check`.
