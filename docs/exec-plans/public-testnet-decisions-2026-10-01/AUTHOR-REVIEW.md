1. **Parameter trust.** Live Cardano parameters are construction authority, not replacement deployment identity. Original reservation policy and basis remain fixed; historical parameter and maximum-capacity rows are authenticated evidence for recovery. Current construction retains wallet, contract, category, economic, and reference-script authority. No validator parameter ABI changed. 2. **Always-succeeds scripts.** No validator arm, blueprint parameter application, yield handshake, or script loader changed. The disposable profile build's compiled validators and definitions match the existing source-fresh blueprint; only profile metadata differs.
3. **Decoders and compiler.** Retrieved payloads use the shared decoder and independently recompute retained commitments and counts. Membership candidates are acquired from the bound native ledger and their authenticated directory graph is checked. Historical funding and trusted-head floors require exact canonical data and an authentication tag. No Aiken decoder changed; the full Aiken gate uses the pinned compiler.
4. **Value conservation.** Funding refresh changes adequate coin selection, while original signed bytes, actual transaction outputs, current collateral percentage and input limits remain guarded. A previously signed two-input intent can be reconciled under a later one-input limit; that history cannot authorize a fresh invalid submission. No unattended bond-refill or registration loop was introduced.
5. **Anchoring.** A peer's self-consistent transport hash does not authorize substituted block content. Retained header commitments are checked separately. Node deletion requires authenticated indexing coverage and its current retained anchor. Archive classification and completion derive from privately admitted exact captures and verified canonical terminal evidence. Trusted-head compaction preserves the authenticated predecessor of the retained suffix.
6. **Reference scripts.** Original contract/reference rosters remain pinned across parameter refresh. No inline production witness fallback or new family deployment was introduced.
7. **Both polarities.** Tests pair accepted payloads with substituted roots/counts, live funding continuation with nonparameter policy substitution refusals, old signed recovery with current submission refusal, eligible retention with active/pending/legacy pins, authentic floors with forged/missing floors, and membership removal with transient/never-active observations. Emulator acceptance includes the existing positive and adversarial installed-script scenarios.
8. **Gates that can fail.** Review found real composition gaps despite earlier unit success: missing history-producer authority; future indexer donations influencing membership capture; current funding caps blocking old signed recovery; stale collateral pricing and capacity cycles; and archive completion/quiet-progress gaps. The item and review documents record targeted regressions and observed mutation failures. Zero collection, fixture refusal before the intended behavior, and sandbox failures are excluded from passing evidence.
9. **Execution and size budgets.** New node download attempts, retry state, paging, and deletion batches have explicit limits. Trusted-head disk history retains a bounded suffix plus authenticated floor. Necessary live evidence remains pinned under capacity pressure. Archive retirement scans within the existing hard store bounds; production-size scan latency and disk-pressure behavior were not measured. No on-chain execution ledger was changed.
10. **TypeScript/Aiken twins.** No canonical codec or semantic proof computation changed, and no golden was regenerated to conceal a mismatch. Required golden channels and execution ledgers check the unchanged encodings against the current build.
11. **Ledger facts.** Membership absence comes from a complete authenticated historical ledger acquisition after finality, not an indexer omission or an old activity marker's age. Recovery uses the actual signed body and canonical inclusion/expiry evidence. Quiet archive progress requires a fresh admitted observation of the exact delivered native point; wall time and fabricated advancement do not grant deletion authority.
12. **Replacement guards.** Removing the boot-time live-parameter equality replaces it with immutable deployment/nonparameter checks plus current construction limits. Historical recovery uses authenticated past capacity while fresh actions use current limits. HTTP activation keeps one scoped listener. Duty cancellation includes scoped Genesis work. Retention preserves head, queue, recovery, pending signed operation, challenge, and supported rollback obligations. Whole linked transcript identities retire atomically; unknown or legacy lifecycle state never becomes deletion permission.
