# B1 — Live testing confirmation depth

Owner ruling (1 October 2026): local-devnet-testing and preprod-testing use 10 L1
confirmations. Emulator-only testing stays at three; mainnet and preprod-public
stay at 30. The ruling also asks for a CI test that adds every bounded wait to
the response budget and checks it against the response window, and for the
numbers to be reported if the waits do not fit under 720 seconds at depth 10.

Changed: both YAML profiles, the regenerated core profile identities, the
pinned expectations in `demo/scripts/deployment-profiles.test.mjs`,
`demo/midgard-node-tools/tests/e2e-release-finality-policy.test.ts` and
`demo/midgard-watcher/tests/runtime/deployment-identity.test.ts`, and
`config/deployments/README.md`. No existing deployment was reset or migrated.

## Response budget

`demo/da-committee-node/tests/availability-response-budget.test.ts` reads every term
from the constant the code runs with. The L1 path is
`minimumDaResponseBudgetMs` from `demo/scripts/deployment-profiles.mjs`:
(confirmation depth + 5 chained publications + 1 poll block) × 20 s × 2. The A1
term is `FOREIGN_DA_RETRY_POLICY.cooldownMs`. The window is
`da_small_response_window_ms`.

| Profile                   | L1 path | A1 cooldown | Total   | Window  | Fits           |
| ------------------------- | ------- | ----------- | ------- | ------- | -------------- |
| local-devnet-testing (10) | 640 s   | 300 s       | 940 s   | 720 s   | no, 220 s over |
| preprod-testing (10)      | 640 s   | 300 s       | 940 s   | 720 s   | no, 220 s over |
| preprod-public (30)       | 1,440 s | 300 s       | 1,740 s | 3,600 s | yes            |
| mainnet (30)              | 1,440 s | 300 s       | 1,740 s | 3,600 s | yes            |

Three named waits have no time bound in the code, so the test lists them and
fails until they get one: the A7 rebuild (one rebuild, no deadline on the
refresh or the rebuild), B6 retries (not implemented) and A5
rebroadcast-before-replace (no exported bound).

The waits do not fit at depth 10, so the depth was not changed again and the
result is returned to the owner. The A1 cooldown is a node-side wait for
foreign payloads; it is not on the committee responder's path.

Increasing depth reduces exposure to shallow rollbacks. It does not replace
authenticated recovery or establish finality.
