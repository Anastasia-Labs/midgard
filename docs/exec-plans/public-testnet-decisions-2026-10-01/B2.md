# B2 — Fund bonds during initial setup, never automatically restore slashing

Decision: automatic funding is permitted only when the bond has never been funded. A low balance is not proof of that state. A restart must not make a formerly funded and subsequently slashed bond look new.

The running node, watcher and committee source contains no recurring DA security-bond top-up maintainer to disable. Existing `da-bond top-up` is an explicit CLI action; acceptance-test top-ups are test scenarios. Reference-script working-capital replenishment is operational funding, a different resource. No automatic bond refill was introduced or run.

Relevant source: [manual command](../../../demo/midgard-node/src/commands/da-bond.ts#L1), [CLI registration](../../../demo/midgard-node/src/index.registration-2.ts#L28), and [reference-script funding](../../../demo/midgard-node/src/transactions/reference-scripts.ensure-reference-script-wallet-working-capital.ts#L105).

Any future unattended setup flow must prove never-funded status using authenticated bond history or durable deployment setup records, persist its exact signed transaction, and reconcile uncertain submission before retrying. It must not infer permission from zero balance, a missing in-memory flag, or a fresh process. Operator re-registration remains explicitly forbidden by A10.

Evidence: source inspection of node/watcher/committee/node-tools top-up paths. No financial transaction, deployment change, or bond reset was made. No new runtime behavior was needed for the absent maintainer; no artificial test was added to assert absence.
