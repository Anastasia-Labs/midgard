\begin{abstract}[-0.5em]{1.5cm}
Midgard is the first optimistic rollup protocol on Cardano.
It is designed to move transaction execution from Layer~1 (L1) to Layer~2 (L2), while settling commitments and disputes through Cardano L1.
Its intended security depends on complete fraud-proof coverage, timely independent challengers, available block data, sound economic parameters, and Cardano settlement assumptions; those conditions must be verified for each deployment.

This whitepaper outlines the interactions between L1 and L2, the role of operators and watchers, and the processes for managing state transitions and resolving disputes.
The document presents a design for increasing throughput while preserving explicit L1-enforced safety conditions.

We discuss the economic incentives that ensure the protocol's integrity and efficiency.
Operators guarantee the validity of their committed blocks by posting bonds, which are slashable if the blocks are proven invalid.
Members of the public are incentivized to watch the operators' blocks, and they are rewarded with a portion of the slashed bonds when their fraud proofs prevent invalid blocks from merging into Midgard's confirmed state.

Midgard's architecture leverages a family of smart contracts on Cardano's L1 to manage state transitions and enforce the security of the L2 operations.
Key components include operator management, compact L1 commitments to L2 blocks, data availability, and a catalogue of fraud-proof procedures.
Overall, Midgard's design aims to provide a scalable and secure solution for the growing needs of the Cardano ecosystem.
\end{abstract}
