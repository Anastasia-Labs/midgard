Chapter 3 specifies how operators take turns producing blocks and how the protocol
stays live and safe when they misbehave. Each component is defined by its on-chain
scripts (typically a minting policy and a spending validator) and its UTxO
representation.

## Components

- **Time model**: how the protocol measures time, rounds, and operator shifts.
- **State queue**: the on-chain queue of committed block headers awaiting
  settlement. The chapter covers its **UTxO representation**, **minting policy**, and
  **spending validator**, and the commit/merge lifecycle.
- **Scheduler**: how block-production duty rotates between operators.
- **Operator directory**: the registry of operators (active, registered, retired)
  maintained as an on-chain linked list.
- **Reserve and payout**: the **reserve spending validator**, **payout minting
  policy**, and **payout spending validator** that hold protocol funds and disburse
  withdrawals.
- **Midgard hub oracle**: the central config/nonce authority, with its own minting
  policy and spending validator.
- **Escape hatch**: the fallback that lets users exit safely if operators stall.

## In the codebase

These map directly to the on-chain
[consensus validators](/docs/onchain/consensus-validators) and
[operator directory](/docs/onchain/operator-directory), to the operator-facing
builders in [`midgard-sdk`](/docs/sdk/midgard-sdk/blocks-and-scheduling), and to the
node's [background fibers](/docs/operators/node/background-fibers) that run the
commit/confirm/merge lifecycle.
