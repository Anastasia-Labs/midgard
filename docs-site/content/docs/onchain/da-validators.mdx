---
title: "Data-Availability Validators"
description: "The da-attestation control plane and da-params-governor validators."
---

Two validators govern Midgard's **data-availability (DA)** control plane on L1,
enforcing the DA rules from the [Off-Chain Data Architecture](/docs/spec/offchain-data-architecture)
chapter.

## Validators

- **`da-attestation`**: the DA attestation control plane. Records committee
  attestations that block data has been made available, gated by a signature
  threshold from the DA committee.
- **`da-params-governor`**: governs the DA parameters (for example the committee
  configuration and thresholds), controlling how those values may change.

<Callout type="info" title="Blueprint note">
These validators exist in the Aiken source, but the committed
`onchain/aiken/plutus.json` blueprint can lag the source on an in-progress branch.
Before you build against them, run `aiken build` in `onchain/aiken` and confirm
that `da_attestation` and `da_params_governor` appear in the blueprint you get.
</Callout>
