The attestation, bond-pool, governance, and availability-challenge validators govern Midgard's **data-availability (DA)** control plane on L1,
enforcing the DA rules from the [Off-Chain Data Architecture](/docs/spec/offchain-data-architecture)
chapter.

Their Aiken/blueprint module identifiers include `da_attestation`,
`da_bond_pool`, `da_params_governor`, and `availability_challenge`.

## Validators

- **`da-attestation`**: the DA attestation control plane. Records committee
  attestations that block data has been made available, gated by a signature
  threshold from the DA committee. Signatures bind the full availability
  commitment. Applying an attestation to the state queue reads the DA bond
  pool as a reference input and needs the pool `Bonded` with at least one
  `da_bond_lovelace` of backing above its floor; the queue node then records
  `Attested` with the commitment hash. Attesting a block locks no bond of its
  own.
- **`da-bond-pool`**: the committee's one pooled DA bond, a single UTxO
  identified by a one-shot NFT that is never burned. Anyone may top it up. Only
  an availability-challenge timeout slashes it, taking
  `da_slash_penalty_lovelace` first. The DA params owner quorum withdraws from
  it in two steps (`BeginWithdraw`, then `CompleteWithdraw` after
  `da_bond_withdraw_delay_ms`), and can cancel a pending withdrawal.
- **`da-params-governor`**: governs the DA parameters (for example the committee
  configuration and thresholds), controlling how those values may change.

- **`availability-challenge`**: governs retained-data challenges against an
  attested block: opening a challenge record funded by the challenger,
  publication tranches, closing on a complete response, and the timeout that
  slashes the DA bond pool and removes the withheld block. Queue availability is
  typed state rather than a policy-id marker. See the
  [committee contract boundary](/docs/watchers/da-committee-node#contract-boundary).

<Callout type="info" title="Blueprint note">
  These validators exist in the Aiken source and in the checked-in
  `onchain/aiken/plutus.json`. After a validator or parameter change, run `aiken
  build --env testnet` in `onchain/aiken`, review the generated diff, and verify
  the deployment manifest uses the intended script hashes.
</Callout>
