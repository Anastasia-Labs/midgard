description: "Building reserve and withdrawal-payout transactions, with their datum and redeemer types."
---

These modules build the transactions that manage the protocol reserve and pay out
withdrawals, enforced on-chain by the
[`reserve` and `payout` validators](/docs/onchain/consensus-validators).

## Modules

- **`reserve`**: the reserve spending redeemer schema, exported as
  `ReserveSpendRedeemer`.
- **`payout`**: schemas for payout state, minting, and spending. Exports `PayoutDatum`,
  `PayoutSpendRedeemer`, and `PayoutMintRedeemer`.
- **`reserve-payout`** (`reserve-payout.ts` plus the `reserve-payout/` directory): the
  transaction builders for deposit absorption, payout initialization/funding,
  and conclusion.
